## What was built
Step 0: tree was clean on `vanilla-sand-sea`; tagged `world-v1.0-base`; new branch `dunes`. The `small-islands` branch was already deleted at the owner's request (not part of 1.0).

All data, generated by `node tools/rewrite/gen.js` from `tools/rewrite/ocean.json` through the new `tools/rewrite/dunes.js`; no `minecraft:` file overridden.
- **Noises** (our namespace): `arrakis_dunes` (first octave −6, amplitudes 1, 0.6, 0.3), `arrakis_dune_fields` (first octave −10, 1, 1, 0.5), `arrakis_dune_style` (first octave −10, 1, 1). No vanilla noise has these sizes.
- **`overworld/dune_height`** (2D, no y):
  `clamp(max_height × field × gate × shape, 0, max_height)`.
  - `field = clamp((F − field_threshold) / field_soft, 0, 1)`, F = the field noise at (x, z) unscaled and unwarped (so the surface rule can test the same noise exactly).
  - `gate = clamp((gate_end − continents) / (gate_end − gate_open), 0, 1)`: 1 at and below −0.60, 0 at −0.18, using our `continents` function.
  - `shape = flat_mult × (sharp_w × sharp + (1 − sharp_w) × smooth)`; `sharp = clamp(1 − 2.8 × |D|)` (a crest line where the dune noise D crosses zero), `smooth = smoothstep(clamp(2.2 × D, 0, 1))` (rounded mound); the style noise S gives `sharp_w` (S above the sharp threshold) and `flat_mult` (0 where S is below the flat threshold). Thresholds come from `dune_sharp_share` / `dune_flat_share` with the measured standard deviation of S (0.336): flat below −0.283, sharp above 0.129.
  - D, S and F are wrapped in `cache_2d` (equal nodes share one NoiseChunk cache) so each is computed once per column.
  - Warp: none by default (`dune_warp_blocks` = 0). The field must stay unwarped so the surface rule can match it exactly, and the vanilla shift noises are cached per 4 blocks (a warp would stair-step the dune shape). The knob exists for the dune shape only.
- **Router term** (outermost): `final_density = max(final_density_vanilla, term)`, `term = range_choice(y ≥ sea_level …, k × (sea_level − 0.5 − y + interpolated(dune_height in the y band)), −1,000,000)`. Surface at `y < sea_level + h − 0.5`: h = 10 gives a top block at sea_level + 9 = 10 above the top sand block; h < 0.5 gives nothing, so a tiny height never makes a one-block sheet. Sea level is read from the noise settings, not hard-coded.
- **Surface rule** (first branch, then the old bedrock and deepslate rules): `y_above(sea_level)` → `not y_above(sea_level + max_height + 2)` → `above_preliminary_surface` → `noise_threshold(arrakis_dune_fields ≥ field_threshold − 0.01)` → `dune_sand`. Final block names: `dune_sand`, `bedrock`, `deepslate` (default block stays stone).
- **DH** (`DhSandGenerator`): reads `overworld/dune_height` from the density function registry and wires its noises to the level's seed with `DensityFunction.mapAll` and a visitor that swaps each `NoiseHolder` for one from `RandomState.getOrCreateNoise` (the way `RandomState` does it). Per ocean column one more 2D evaluation; where the height is at least 1 the sand goes up `ceil(h − 0.5)` blocks above sea level. Same guarding as before: any failure or a missing function gives `null` and flat sand, as today.
- **Knobs** in `ocean.json` (each with a `_notes` entry, all old keys kept): `dune_enabled`, `dune_max_height`, `dune_wavelength`, `dune_field_scale`, `dune_field_threshold`, `dune_field_soft`, `dune_style_scale`, `dune_sharp_share`, `dune_flat_share`, `dune_gate_open`, `dune_gate_end`, `dune_warp_blocks`, plus `dune_term_slope` (k = 1).
- Tools: `tools/model/NoiseStat.java` (feature sizes), `nodes.js` (node count), `timing.sh` (A/B timing).

## Answers to the prompt's questions
1. **Does `range_choice` evaluate only the active branch, and the real cost?** Yes (source above). Router term: 8 nodes (2 `range_choice`, 3 `y_clamped_gradient`, `mul`, `add`, 1 `interpolated`). `dune_height`: 51 nodes, 7 of them cached noise reads (equal nodes share one cache), so 3 noise evaluations per column: dunes 3 octaves, fields 3, style 2, plus the `continents` flat-cache read. Where evaluated: the height is evaluated at cell corners only (every 4 blocks in x and z) and only for corner y between 56 and 88 (the corner band around 63 to 74); elsewhere the interpolated corner is 0 with no noise. Per block: one gradient + compare, and for y from sea level to about sea level + 11 one interpolated read and a multiply.
2. **Interpolation across the sea-level boundary?** The term is not interpolated in y: only the height h is (constant in y), the linear part `sea_level − 0.5 − y` is exact per block, and the outer `range_choice` (y ≥ sea_level) is per block. So nothing at or below `sea_level − 1` is touched, and nothing becomes solid below sea level. The fill below stays exactly as before.
3. **Surface rule coverage and edge?** Every dune block is inside the rule region (density mask zero at noise ≤ threshold; rule region from threshold − 0.01), so no stone rim by construction, and the cap `sea_level + max_height + 2` is above any dune. **But** the rule also converts land rock inside the field and band: see Surprises.
4. **Share the noises later?** Yes: the field noise (unscaled, surface-rule readable) can place rockberg or similar decoration; the style noise and dune noise are plain noises usable from other density functions. For a wind direction: not directly (no direction in any of them by design).
5. **Carvers cutting dunes?** Yes, the carvers can: `#minecraft:overworld_carver_replaceables` includes the sand tag and Dune Sand is in `minecraft:sand`; the carver's liquid check only looks at water/lava fluids, not sand. Caves that reach the surface can open into dunes as they do into stone; I saw no reason to stop them (same as the stone terrain).

Cost numbers: A/B of 21 × 21 chunks around 0,0 (two `forceload` commands, polled every 0.3 s for the first success of "if loaded" on five chunks, seed 2, new world each time, dev server, nothing else running; JVM start each run):

| run | dunes off | dunes on |
|---|---|---|
| first (before `cache_2d`) | 1.8, 1.8 s | 1.9, 2.1, 2.1 s |
| final (with `cache_2d`) | 1.9, 1.8 s | 2.0, 2.0 s |

Final: about 1.85 s off, 2.0 s on, +8%, but the poll resolution is 0.3 s, so the difference is within noise: the cost is at or below roughly 10%. Without the `cache_2d` wrap it was about +15%. If it needs to go lower: fewer octaves in `arrakis_dunes` and `arrakis_dune_fields`, drop the style noise (fixed mix), or coarser fields.

## Surprises, problems, deviations from the prompt
- **Land rock in dune fields.** A surface rule cannot ask for the dune height or the continentalness (only noises at unscaled block coordinates, y, preliminary surface, biome, stone depth). Stone depth does not help either: the sea fill below a dune counts as stone, so a dune and a land column both look like one long solid. So the rule region is "inside a field, y between sea level and sea level + 11, above the preliminary surface" and it cannot exclude land. Where low land (preliminary surface roughly below y 78) lies inside a dune field, its rock between sea level and sea level + 11 that is above the preliminary surface (about 4 blocks under it) turns into Dune Sand: coasts and low islands in fields get sandy beaches instead of stone tops. Taller land and everything outside fields is unaffected, and the density, DH and ocean are unaffected. The only exact fix is data-driven by biome (a second biome in a multi_noise source, with a biome condition in the rule); that changes the biome source and costs per-chunk climate sampling, so I did not do it. Owner's call: accept the sandy low coasts in fields, or approve the biome route.
- 30% of positions at the continentalness cap lie below sea level in the islands branch; not relevant here.
- Interpolating the height (4-block bilinear) rather than evaluating it per block: much cheaper, and equal for 60+ block features.
- No block probe or look check: a `/fill`-based count was refused by the permission classifier.
- Cost: the first version (no `cache_2d`) was about +15%; wrapping the repeated noise nodes brought it to about +8% (within the timing resolution).
