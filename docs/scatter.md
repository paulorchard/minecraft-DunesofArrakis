# Scattered footprint, stone base, lower dunes (prompt 12)

Three switches in `data/islandcraft_dunesofarrakis/arrakis/world.json` (read when a world loads; restart the world/server to change them):

| key | default | off value |
|---|---|---|
| `footprint` | `"scatter"` | `"mapped"` = the 104 mapped ellipses of prompt 11 |
| `materials` | `"new"` | `"old"` = prompt 11 block choice |
| `dune_height_scale` | `0.33` | `1.0` = prompt 11 dunes |

`footprint: mapped` + `materials: old` + `dune_height_scale: 1.0` reproduces prompt 11 exactly: 38 windows (prompt 07-11 windows incl. islands, gaps, edges, plus the kind-override runs 1/2/3), **0 differing blocks, 0 differing columns** (`tools/worldscan/windiff.js`).

## A. Scattered footprint

**Method (`ScatterGenerator`).** One deterministic table per world, built the first time a world's noise is seen (cached per world in `WorldModels`, about 1.0-1.4 s, ±150 km square, about 14,000 masses, 15,000 ellipses). It is a pure function of the world seed hash (`IslandGenerator.seedOf(noise, salt)`) and the config: per-level hash-based candidates in 24 km / 6 km / 2 km cells, thinned by a very slow density noise, then accepted in priority order (spawn island, first destination, large, medium, small, islets, higher level first, ties by hash) only if the exact ellipse-to-ellipse distance to everything already placed is at least `gap_nominal` (170 m, so at least 100 m remain after the coast noise); satellites are placed after their parent, 150-500 m off its coast. Nothing depends on which chunk is generated first, so generation order, direction and the number of times a window is generated cannot matter: the table does not depend on requests, only on the seed. Checked: same seed twice 0 differing blocks, reverse tile order 0 differing blocks (6 windows, 2 seeds).

**Finite extent.** ±150 km (`extent_half`), so the table is bounded; outside it the world is open dune sand. (A table is needed because the gap rule is global; extent could be raised at about 1 s per 300 km square.)

**Landmass** = one or more rotated ellipses (large = chain of 1-6 overlapping lobes). Footprint goes through the same `ContinentIndex` -> `CoastModel` -> `ReliefModel` -> `ToughModel` path as mapped ellipses. Test (`scatter.sh testmode`): masses generated by the scatter and the same ellipses placed alone in a mapped-style index give bit-identical field, R', base top and Rt (8000 points, 0 differing). No sixth size class was needed: lobes keep each ellipse at most about 3 km semi-axis, so the existing classes (upper limits 350/700/1400 m, small below 120 m) apply.

**How a later layer reads the footprint:** `WorldModels.shared().of(noise)` gives `.index` (ellipses), `.coast` (field), `.relief`, `.tough` and `.scatter` (masses, kinds, levels); one call to the coast field says where land is.

**Config (`world.json` `scatter` block)** — see the file; main numbers: density noise wavelength 10 km (+4.3 km at weight 0.35, gain 3.2, max multiplier 2.6); large 24 km cells, 2.6 per cell, 4-15 km, lobes up to 6; medium 6 km cells, 1.1 per cell, 1-4 km; small 2 km cells, 0.55 per cell, 80 m-1 km; islets 1.5 km cells, 0.0 per cell (off), 60-200 m; satellites 75% of large, 40% of medium, up to 3 each, 150-500 m off the parent, 30% clusters; minor/major 0.4-0.9; kinds by a 8 km (+3.2 km) noise thresholded at ±0.16 at the mass centre, satellites inherit the parent's kind, `kind_override` (null, 1, 2, 3) still works.

**Spawn island.** Always at the origin, id 9000, 250-500 m, kind 0, noise scaled to 0.2 so the rock outline stays clean. The world spawn of a freshly generated world is read from `level.dat`: (0, 113, 0) on seed 1 and (0, 108, 0) on seed 2, i.e. on the island, so vanilla spawn logic already lands the player there and nothing was changed. A first destination (small/medium island 480-1,100 m away) is forced.

### Results (field tools, 20 seeds, `docs/scatter/sweep_20_seeds.txt`; windows below on seeds 1 and 2)

* Land share of the 50 km square: min 8.2% (seed 6), **median 12.3%**, max 16.7% (seed 10). The median is in 12-20%; four to five seeds fall under 12%. A density knob (`per_cell`) moves the whole range.
* Large masses in the square: min 4 (seed 6), median 10, max 13; largest large mass 14.5-15.0 km across on every seed. Medium 46-67 and small+satellite 330-420 in the square on seeds 1-3.
* Density unevenness: 5x5 grid of 10 km cells, max/min ratio min 17 (seed 9), median 287; cells under 4% land per seed min 3, median 6, max 14.
* Gap after coast noise between different masses: min 100.1 m (seed 5), median 103.6 m. **0 accidental merges on all 20 seeds.** (One extra effect: the noise can cut a thin neck of one large mass in two, 1-3 fragments per seed, down to 40-100 m between the pieces; they are one landmass by design, not an accidental merge.)
* Spawn island: present on every seed, rock clearance from the origin min 77 m (seed 14); nearest other land min 510 m (seed 11), median 772, max 916; kind 0 on all.
* Largest empty circle (no land): 10.1-23.5 km across, median 14.9 km.
* Kinds: masses are balanced (about 4,300-5,500 of each, a third each), but the share of land *area* per kind varies by seed (e.g. seed 1 0.29/0.48/0.24; seed 9 0.11/0.22/0.67) because a few large masses dominate area. Median kind-zone diameter 2.6-7.4 km (typically about 5 km).

## B. Stone base, sandstone skin, tough rock from it

`materials: new`. Base rock: the top `sandstone_skin` (3) blocks below the base surface are sandstone, then stone with blobs, deepslate and bedrock as prompt 09. Tough rock (kinds 1-3): sandstone foot fading into stone (3-8 blocks, ragged by noise), stone body with andesite bands (kind 3: stripes 1-5, kind 2: 3-10, seeded, offset by slow noise), small andesite patches on tops, stone caps; kind 1 keeps its vertical andesite streaks. Settings are `materials_new` in `tough.json`. Heights are not touched: old vs new materials, footprint fixed, scale 1.0: **0 differing heights** on 6 windows.

Allowed blocks inside the footprint: sand, sandstone, stone, andesite, diorite, granite, tuff, calcite, deepslate, bedrock (plus the mod's dune sand). Checked on 20 scatter windows (`matcheck.js`): no red sandstone, no terracotta; the only exceptions are 20 and 15 `smooth_sandstone` blocks in the two medium windows, at the outline columns that the outline check lists as 1 block off the coast (inferred: the open-desert layering at a column the footprint mask counts as inside, not rock). 0 air below the surface in every window. Base columns: sandstone exactly 3 then stone/blob on 99.97-99.99% of columns (the exceptions, 0.01-0.04%, are the same 1-block rock-top differences as the relief mismatch). Exposed stone on a base surface: 3 columns in 20 windows (2 in kind1 s1, 1 in dest s2).

Tough-rock foot (kind 1/2/3 windows): sandstone share by block above the base top about 90/75/50/25/8/2/0%: **below 50% at h 3-4, below 5% at h 6** (kind 3 mesa sample: below 50% at 2, below 5% at 4). The per-column unbroken sandstone foot is 1-4 blocks, ragged (p10/p50/p90 = 1/3/4). One full face column per kind is printed in `docs/scatter/t12_sc_*_mat.txt`; slices old vs new in `docs/scatter/slices/` (spawn island, base coast, foot and face of each kind).

## C. Lower dunes

`dune_height_scale` multiplies every dune excursion above y 63 (dune function value times scale, both density calls in `ArrakisSurface`); sand level, wavelengths, positions and orientation are not touched; `max(Rt, dune)` stays. 1.0 = prompt 11 exactly (regression above). Three open-sand 1 km windows (`docs/scatter/dune_stats.txt`):

| window | p50 | p95 | max | share >20 m | share within ±1 of y 63 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| open desert, 1.0 / 0.33 | 32 / 11 | 86 / 28 | 113 / 37 | 67.8% / 21.5% | 1.0% / 7.0% |
| sc_bare s1, 1.0 / 0.33 | 28 / 10 | 86 / 28 | 108 / 36 | 60.3% / 24.3% | 2.3% / 9.8% |
| sc_bare s2, 1.0 / 0.33 | 26 / 9 | 94 / 31 | 119 / 39 | 59.3% / 17.3% | 2.1% / 8.8% |

p95 and max are a third. The shape is the same field, scaled: |h(0.33) − 0.33·h(1.0)| averages 0.26-0.53 blocks, correlation 0.9994 (the window with 0.975 includes a land mass). The highest point per 64 m cell moves a median 4-5 blocks (flat crest tops, ties), p90 17-27. Flag: the flat share (within ±1 of y 63) rises from 1-2% to 7-10%, as expected when heights shrink to a third; no new pits. Wavelength / height ratio by the crude transect finder in `dunestats.js` (noisy): about 5-11 at 1.0 and 3-4 at 0.33; the real ratio simply triples because only the height changed.

## Window and chunk checks (real chunks, 1024×1024, seeds 1 and 2)

Windows: sc_spawn, sc_dest, sc_large, sc_kind1/2/3, sc_medium, sc_small, sc_dense, sc_bare. `runServer` generates all with no error or warning naming the mod; `gradlew build` passes.

* Mismatch of the relief vs analytic: 0 to 0.019% of columns (all < 0.1%), no air below the surface in any window, outline vs field outline: 0-2 differing columns (all within one block of the coast), floating sand 0-10 blocks in a window (isolated blocks at the rock/dune contact, the same effect measured in earlier prompts).
* 0 differing columns across seed-twice and reverse-order runs.
* Cost (ms per chunk, same session): open desert/bare 6.0 (prompt 11 build 6.4; the new build with every switch off 6.7); spawn island 6.0 (6.7 old origin); dense 6.0-7.5; large landmass 7.5-8.4 (old slice_green 9.0); kind 3 cliff 7.5-8.0 (old 8.5), kind 1 7.3-7.8 (old spire heart 9.7). Open-desert ratio new/old about 0.94 (mapped switches-off build 1.05). Footprint lookup is the existing ContinentIndex with 256 m tile flags; the scatter table adds a one-off 1.0-1.4 s per world.
* Visibility (22 masses, partial sample from the 20 windows): masses more than 80% dune-covered: 1 of 22 (a 326 m satellite, not visible at all); smallest visible island 98 m across, peak 14 m above the sand (dune p95 at 0.33 is 28 m, so small islands are often partly buried); large/medium/small all mostly visible (median covered 3%/5%/9%). Dune mountains still overlap rock occasionally (up to 20 m at the coast on seed 2).

## Open questions (reported, not decided)

* Continents? The largest masses are about 15 km across and have the full coast, ramp and terrace structure; whether they read as continents needs a walk (teleport below).
* 100 m gap: the numbers above; the largest empty circle is 10-23 km.
* One regional kind per large landmass: kind-zone diameter 2.6-7.4 km is smaller than a 15 km landmass, so a large mass takes the kind at its centre only. A second noise per lobe would be cheap; not built.
* Exposed stone on base surface: 3 columns across 20 windows.
